# tene Plan

Author and revise the plan in the user's current conversation language, following the workflow language contract.

Require an active Sprint in `plan`, then load its context pack. Break confirmed criteria into vertical tasks with dependencies, Understanding Layer, expected artifacts, verification, rollback, and policy decisions. Link each task to an intent or acceptance criterion.

Record alternatives and reject scope that is not needed for the confirmed outcomes. Keep unresolved policy choices visible instead of guessing. Update and validate the plan document, add tasks through the CLI, then dry-run the design transition.

Read [workflow](../../references/workflow.md) and [CLI reference](../../references/cli.md).
